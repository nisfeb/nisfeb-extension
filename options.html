Your ship from any page. Everything here talks to the ship over its own authenticated HTTP API, with the same session cookie the ship's web apps use.

The origin your ship serves on. Every request is built from it and nothing else.

Used once and never stored. It buys a session cookie from the ship, and the cookie is what the browser keeps. A ship restart invalidates it: connect again then.

Pages sent to Orrery are then written as this key, so every fact carries the extension's own name and the key's scope bounds what it may file. Mint one in Orrery under Clients, with write. It is kept in this browser and used for nothing else. Blank: pages are sent as you, with the session cookie.

Turns urb:// addresses and furum's f/~host/board into links on the pages you read: a furum board opens on your ship, and an urb:// link keeps its own address (for Lattice or Talon on this computer) with a ↗ beside it to open it in Lattice on your ship. A script has to run on every page for this, so turning it on asks the browser for access to all sites. The script only reads page text and adds links; it sends nothing anywhere. Turning it off stops the script; the site access stays until you remove it in the browser's extension settings.

A page with today's events, unread chats and mentions, open Orrery actions, mail waiting and your Armillary balance, with a reply box. The Today button in the popup opens it. To make it the new tab in Brave, two settings, both yours to change back: under Appearance, turn on Show home button and give it the address above as its custom address; under Get started, set New tab page shows to Homepage. Choose Dashboard there to have Brave's own new tab back. The extension never replaces the new tab itself. Chrome has no such setting, so in Chrome the page opens from the popup.
