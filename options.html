Turns urb:// addresses and furum's f/~host/board into links on the pages you read: a furum board opens on your ship, and an urb:// link keeps its own address (for Lattice or Talon on this computer) with a ↗ beside it to open it in Lattice on your ship. A script has to run on every page for this, so turning it on asks the browser for access to all sites. The script only reads page text and adds links; it sends nothing anywhere. Turning it off stops the script; the site access stays until you remove it in the browser's extension settings.

The day page

Your day, drawn as Talon's home page: its sky clock, today's events, Orrery's open actions and its spend this month, the mail waiting and your Armillary balance. The Today button in the popup opens it. To arrange the cards, right-click or long-press any of them on the page, as in Talon, then drag them or use their arrows, and press Done.

Browsing history into Orrery

Each hour, the sites you visited, how many pages of each, and the pages' titles go to Orrery's read channel on your ship, under the Orrery key above when one is set. Orrery reads it as it reads a message and files what matters. Never a page's text, never your ship's own pages, never a site listed below, and never a private window's, which the browser keeps out of its history. Turning this on asks the browser to let the extension read its history. If Orrery's model is a cloud model, that model reads the digest.

Use Armillary in Brave Leo

Leo can answer with your Armillary models through its Bring your own model form. The extension cannot fill that form itself: Brave keeps it in its own settings, out of any extension's reach. So here are its values, each to copy and paste.

To make it the new tab in Brave, two settings, both yours to change back: under Appearance, turn on Show home button and give it the address above as its custom address; under Get started, set New tab page shows to Homepage. Choose Dashboard there to have Brave's own new tab back. The extension never replaces the new tab itself. Chrome has no such setting, so in Chrome the page opens from the popup.
